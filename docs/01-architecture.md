# 01 · Architecture decision: the September 23 build of Roster Finance

Prism PLAN run, 2026-09-17. Lean fleet: first-principles, adversary, practitioner, security, regulatory, plus two grounding probes (mainnet mint reads; Pyth, Surfpool, Anchor, Squads verification). Skeptic pass: 2× Opus + 1× Sonnet on the four load-bearing claims (results in §7). Evidence tiers per claim in §6.

> Cross-tier verification reduces instance- and tier-level error correlation but not shared-lineage blind spots. Treat cross-tier survival as weaker evidence than grounding.

## 1. Recommendation

Build Part 2's pooled-series program with four corrections the panel proved necessary, on the toolchain the same author shipped Lookthrough with (Anchor 1.2.0, anchor-spl `token_interface` + `token_2022_extensions`, hermetic Rust tests on litesvm 0.16 against a synthetic NVDAx replica mint, one surfpool 1.0 fork run on the real mint), one Node `services` process on the fork (price service, quoter, keeper, indexer on `node:sqlite` behind a `Store` interface, REST), the existing Next 16 app reading that REST and building transactions client-side with `signTransaction` only. The corrections: **(a)** assignment happens at exercise time through a stability-pool style product `P` with scale and epoch counters (the Liquity pattern), never pro rata at expiry, and both legs of a writer's settlement are floored independently with the residue forfeited as dust; **(b)** contracts are denominated in *lots* of `10^decimals` raw units and the on-chain strike is `strike_usdc_per_lot`, a `u64` the program stores as given and never derives, so the program reads no multiplier anywhere (Part 1 §4.1 taken literally) and only the USDC leg rounds; **(c)** every writer's state lives in a slot inside the Series account (32 slots, matching the 32 asks), premiums accrue there and are pulled, so `buy` touches a fixed account set; **(d)** `close_series` never waits for position-mint supply to reach zero. Mainnet deploy lands **Sep 21 evening** with a **Sep 22 16:00 NY expiry** on the grid, because a Friday expiry cannot settle on the tape before submission. Everything in addendum B's deferred list is absent from the UI.

## 2. Why (load-bearing reasons)

1. **Part 2 §3's assignment rule is exploitable as written** (adversary, worked numerically). Settling `sold_raw / total_sold_raw × (collateral + settlement)` at expiry lets a writer who enters after exercises have filled the settlement vault take proceeds from earlier writers: A sells 100 calls at K=180, all exercised (18,000 USDC in settlement); B deposits 100 tokens at spot 150 and self-fills at ~0 premium; at expiry each gets 50 tokens + 9,000 USDC; B converted 7,500 of tokens into 9,000 and A lost 1,500. None of §3's three invariants detects it. Listed options assign at exercise among then-open shorts. The fix keeps everything else in §3 and is the Liquity stability-pool accounting: the Series holds a product `P` (1e18 fixed point, starts 1e18), a `scale` and an `epoch`. On `exercise(q)` with `U` unassigned: if `q == U`, `epoch += 1`, `P = 1e18`, `scale = 0`; else `P ← P·(U−q)/U`, and when `P < 1e9` the scale bumps and `P` is multiplied by 1e18 (so `P` never underflows to zero and never overflows). Each writer slot stores `open` (unassigned lots at its last fold), `P_snap`, `scale_snap`, `epoch_snap`, `assigned` and `premium_claimable`. Folding a slot to the present: `unassigned = 0` if `epoch_snap < epoch`; else `floor(open·P/P_snap)` divided by 1e18 per scale step; `assigned += floor(open·(P_snap−P_adj)/P_snap)`; both floors are independent, so `assigned + unassigned ≤ open` and the residue is dust on both legs, never a short vault. A fill of `n` folds first, then `open = unassigned + n` with fresh snapshots. `settle_writer` pays `unassigned × unit_collateral` from the collateral vault, `assigned × strike_usdc_per_lot / 1e6` (floored) from the settlement vault, plus free collateral. The skeptic pass (§7) found three ways the draft's single scalar failed; this formulation is the one all three counterexamples are re-run against in the P1 test list. Addendum F's equal-payout test still passes for writers present at both exercises; new tests: a late writer receives none of earlier proceeds; a full exercise followed by a new writer; the three-round griefing sequence; the two-writer one-unit exercise where the last settler must not revert.
2. **The program is the critical path and the toolchain is proven.** Lookthrough (7 instructions, 26 litesvm tests, web app) shipped in ~2 days on this exact stack; litesvm 0.16 bundles `spl_token_2022` 11.0.0 with ScaledUiAmount and Pausable, so the whole §4.2 + addendum test list runs hermetically in seconds; anchor-syn 1.2 parses `extensions::metadata_pointer::*` on `init` mints and anchor-spl exposes `token_metadata_initialize`, so the position mint is a known pattern (`anchor-syn-1.2.0/src/parser/accounts/constraints.rs:97-260`, `anchor-spl-1.2.0/src/token_2022_extensions/token_metadata.rs:13-45`). Every day of services and frontend is idle until the IDL exists, so P1 starts the moment P0's mint read is in `docs/MINT.md`.
3. **The mint facts are now grounded and favourable.** All 11 launch-set xStocks mints: Token-2022, 8 decimals, extensions `MetadataPointer, PermanentDelegate, DefaultAccountState(Initialized), ScaledUiAmountConfig, PausableConfig(paused=false), ConfidentialTransferMint, TransferHook(program=None, authority set), TokenMetadata`, freeze authority present, **no transfer fee, no live hook** (`fixtures/probes/mint_probe_results.json`, slot 447797526). Plain `transfer_checked` works today; hook extra-account resolution is a P8 path behind a registry rule that fails closed if `TransferHook.program_id` ever becomes non-zero. Tessera mints: `TransferFeeConfig` 20 bps uncapped + freeze authority, nothing else. PreStocks: 50 bps fee since epoch 1032, permanent delegate, pausable, one key holding every authority.
4. **Pull payments close two attacks and one race at once.** Pushing premium to up to 8 writer ATAs inside `buy` (Part 2 §3 wording) means a writer who closes or freezes their USDC ATA blocks the whole book at ask 1 (adversary 5b; security lens listed it as its own known gap), and the remaining-accounts list changes between simulation and signature (the dominant "quote moved" cause). Accruing `premium_claimable` on the writer's slot inside the Series (the skeptics showed a separate `WriterAccount` would put the race back through the state writes) keeps `buy` at a fixed account set, is economically identical for the writer, and lets the writer sweep with `claim_premium` at any time. Logged as a deviation from §3's wording in `DECISIONS.md`.

## 3. Steelman of the rejected options

**Per-commitment escrow (Part 1 §4.2).** Strongest case: no pooled-assignment math at all, no settlement vault, each writer's outcome is exactly their own contract, and it is what Part 1's copy ("the exact escrow account backing the contract") literally promises. Rejected because Part 2 §0 supersedes it for the reasons it gives (routing does not scale, positions must be fungible and visible in wallets) and because the accounting in reason 1 gives per-writer exactness inside the pooled model.

**Pro-rata at expiry, as Part 2 wrote it.** Strongest case: simplest possible `settle_writer`, no state touched on exercise, and addendum F's argument that writers should not care *when* exercises happen. Rejected: it is precisely the "when" that lets a late entrant skim (reason 1); the OCC analogy addendum F relies on assigns at exercise, not at expiry.

**Position mint with 6 decimals and supply = raw units (Part 2 §3 wording).** Strongest case: no unit conversion anywhere. Rejected: NVDAx is 8-decimal, so 180 USDC/share is 1.8 micro-USDC per raw unit, which is not representable as an integer without a fixed-point scale that then puts a division into every exercise (adversary 3b). Lots (`10^decimals` raw = one whole token at multiplier 1, position token 6 dp = fractional lots) make `strike_usdc_per_lot` an exact `u64` and `raw = qty_lot6 × 10^(decimals−6)` exact for every listed mint (8 and 9 decimals).

**Cashless auto-exercise (keeper fronts the strike, swaps via Jupiter, repays itself).** Strongest case: it is the only design under which a call holder who does not keep the full strike in USDC at expiry, or who bought the token on Jupiter, gets auto-exercised. Deferred to P8: it adds a Jupiter CPI/ALT path to the crank on the critical path. For Sep 23: opt-in `enable_auto_exercise` approves a program PDA on both the position ATA and the cash or underlying ATA for the required amounts, and Manage states in plain words what the wallet must hold at expiry and that tokens moved out of the wallet are not auto-exercised.

**Separate quoter/keeper/indexer processes and Postgres from day one (Part 2 §5).** Strongest case: production shape from the start, no later split. Rejected for the fork: one Hermes cache, one DB writer, one restart path; each module keeps its own loop and keypair so splitting later is moving files, and SQLite behind a `Store` with portable SQL is a schema port plus a replay from slot, not a migration.

## 4. The design, concretely

**Accounts** (all with `_reserved: [u8; 64]`):
- `Protocol [b"protocol"]`: authority (Squads vault), pause_authority (hot key, pause-only), treasury, usdc_mint, fee_bps, integrator_share_bps, paused_all, keeper_fee_usdc, grace_secs.
- `MarketConfig [b"market", mint]`: mint, token_program, decimals, extension flags derived on-chain from the mint (`has_transfer_fee`, `has_permanent_delegate`, `pausable`, `hook_program: Option<Pubkey>`), token_feed_id, equity_feed_id, multiplier_source, `allowed_expiries: [i64; 4]` (rolled by the keeper via `update_market`, no DST math on-chain), strike_step_1e6, `max_live_series`, live_series, min_lots, max_lots, max_collateral_per_writer, tier, listed, paused, `issuer_paused_at`.
- `Series [b"series", market, side, strike_usdc_per_lot, expiry_ts]`: bump, collateral_vault `[b"cvault", series]`, settlement_vault `[b"svault", series]`, quote_vault `[b"qvault", series]`, position_mint `[b"pmint", series]` (Token-2022, 6 dp, MetadataPointer + TokenMetadata, MintCloseAuthority = series, mint authority = series), `strike_usdc_per_lot: u64` (micro-USDC per lot of `10^decimals` raw; stored as given, must be a multiple of `market.strike_step`, never derived from a multiplier), `total_sold`, `total_exercised`, `total_collateral`, `P: u128` (1e18), `scale: u8`, `epoch: u32`, `unassigned`, `dust_usdc`, `dust_underlying`, state (Open | Expired | Halted), `halted_at`, `rent_payer`, `seq`, `asks: [Ask; 32]` (`writer_slot: u8, remaining_lots6, ask_per_lot, seq`), `writers: [WriterSlot; 32]` (`writer: Pubkey, deposited, withdrawn, open, P_snap, scale_snap, epoch_snap, assigned, premium_claimable, settled: bool`), settle snapshot fields. About 6 KB, roughly 0.045 SOL, reclaimed by `close_series`; measured in `docs/RENT.md`. A 33rd writer is refused with a clear error; the quoter fleet reserves slots. A slot with `open == 0`, no resident asks, no claimable premium and `deposited == withdrawn` is recyclable.
- No separate `WriterAccount`: the skeptic pass showed per-writer PDAs put up to 8 state writes into `buy`'s account list and reopened the simulate-versus-sign race the design was meant to close.
- `FeeVault` ATA of Protocol per quote mint; `IntegratorClaim [b"integrator", pubkey]` deferred to P9, `referrer: Option<Pubkey>` on `buy` from day one (errors `IntegratorNotRegistered` when Some).
- `AutoExercise [b"autoex", holder]`: enabled, min_itm_bps; the delegate PDA `[b"autoex_authority"]` signs the crank.

**Instructions**: `init_protocol`, `update_protocol`, `create_market(mint, feeds, grid, caps, tier)` (registry authority; derives token program, decimals and extension flags from the mint; rejects a non-canonical quote mint), `update_market`, `pause_market` / `pause_all` (pause key), `unpause_*` (authority), `create_series(side, strike_usdc_per_lot, expiry_ts)` (permissionless within the grid: expiry in `allowed_expiries`, strike a multiple of `strike_step` inside `[min_strike, max_strike]` that the keeper rolls; `live_series < max_live_series`; the program reads no multiplier: the quoter chooses per-lot strikes off-chain from the live multiplier and the xStocks API `reason` field, and the app shows strike per share as `strike_usdc_per_lot / live multiplier`), `quote(lots, ask)` (claims or reuses the writer's slot, deposits, requires `lots ≤ free` where `free = deposited − withdrawn − open − assigned − resident` and `resident` is summed from the ask array on the spot, sorted insert, the worst ask evicted in place when full so its writer's collateral is free again with no drift), `cancel_ask(seq)` (own asks only), `claim_premium`, `withdraw_unsold(lots)` bounded by `free`, `buy(lots6, max_premium_per_lot, referrer)` (walk ≤ 8 asks, each fill ≤ `ask.remaining` which `quote` already collateralised, taker fee to FeeVault, premium accrues on the writer slot, fold-then-add on the slot, mint_to; touches Series, three vaults, position mint, the buyer's two ATAs, FeeVault and the two token programs, nothing chosen by book state; partial fill returns the filled amount; zero fill → `QuoteMoved`), `exercise(lots6)` (burn; owed rounds up, received rounds down; updates `P`, `scale`, `epoch`; never reads a price; never pausable), `enable_auto_exercise` / `disable_auto_exercise`, `auto_exercise(lots6)` (window `[expiry − grace, expiry)`, `PriceUpdateV2` via `pyth-solana-receiver-sdk` 2.0.0 `pro-compatible`, posted in the same transaction with the receiver's `post_update_atomic` (the partially-verified path, which yields `VerificationLevel::Full` on the pro receiver's 5-guardian set at 3 signatures), checked with `get_price_no_older_than(clock, max_age, &market.token_feed_id)`, `conf·10_000 ≤ price·max_conf_bps`, the per-share comparison using the multiplier read from the underlying mint in that instruction only; keeper fee paid from FeeVault so no fee is ever taken from the holder; trust caveat disclosed in `/risk`: that "Full" is Pyth's 3-of-5 guardian set, not Wormhole's 13-of-19), `settle_writer` (after expiry; folds the slot, pays free collateral, then `unassigned × unit_collateral` from the collateral vault and `floor(assigned × strike_usdc_per_lot / 1e6)` from the settlement vault for calls, the mirror for puts; from counters, never from balances), `close_series` (after expiry + grace; vaults empty or dust-only; dust swept to FeeVault; closes vaults and series; closes the mint only if supply is zero), `withdraw_fees` (to `protocol.treasury` ATA only). Events via `#[event_cpi]` (an 8-fill buy overflows the 10 KB log budget with `emit!`).

**Invariants, restated** (checked at every instruction boundary in the property test): `collateral_vault ≥ unassigned × unit_collateral + Σ free + dust`; `settlement_vault ≥ Σ floor(assigned_i × strike / 1e6)` for calls (mirror for puts); `position_mint.supply = total_sold − total_exercised` while Open (no supply condition after expiry); `Σ slot.open_folded = unassigned` within one lot6 unit per slot; `Σ (assigned_i + unassigned_i) ≤ total_sold`, the difference being dust.

**Halt rule**: any instruction that observes `PausableConfig.paused` or a frozen vault sets `halted_at`; `settle_writer` refuses until `unhalt + 24 h`; `exercise` stays open through that window. Transfer-hook rejection is not observable ahead of time: proven on the fork per Part 2 §2, disclosed.

**Authority split** (security lens): Squads vault = upgrade authority (24 h timelock at P5) + `Protocol.authority`; pause key can pause and nothing else; keeper and quoter keys have no on-chain privilege; every vault signs with the series PDA and every vault is `has_one` on the series; every writer or holder destination is derived, never passed.

**Off-chain**: `packages/core` (units, session clock, payoff math, zod), `packages/sdk` (`@anchor-lang/core` client, PDAs, tx builders), `packages/oracle` (Hermes at `https://pyth.dourolabs.app/hermes` with `Authorization: Bearer`, TTL cache; Benchmarks vol 7/30/90 d; session state from the published schedule since `.PRE/.POST/.ON` left Core on 2026-06-15; multiplier watcher), `packages/quoter` (vol floor, session multipliers 1.0/1.4/1.8/2.2, activation window 3.0 or pause, forward = price × new_multiplier/multiplier when an activation precedes expiry, q = 0), `packages/keeper` (auto_exercise, settle, close, grid roll, multiplier watcher), `packages/indexer` (`getSignaturesForAddress` poll + `EventParser`, `node:sqlite` `Store`), `packages/services` (one process, REST on :8787: roster, markets, series, protection per series, positions by wallet). Web: `rosterData()` → `fetch(SERVICES_URL)`, tx builders return unsigned transactions, `/api/tx/send` submits via the app RPC.

**Compliance before P5** (regulatory lens): edge geo gate in `middleware.ts` (US and territories, UK, CA, AU, OFAC comprehensive list, Tessera's named list) on the `(app)` group and every quote/tx API; signed wallet self-attestation on first connect; `/risk`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/fees` pages; the copy FIX list applied (Tessera's redemption is a 90-day window once opened, not "no deadline"; "delivers the token" not "the stock"; "buy NVDAx" not "buy Nvidia"; "Nothing else on earth" softened or dropped; Backpack mints on HOLD until their terms are read).
